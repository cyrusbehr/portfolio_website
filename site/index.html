Positioning without GPS

Tern works out where a vehicle is from its own onboard sensors, on road and off. No satellites, no cell signal, no outside infrastructure, so it keeps working where GPS is jammed, spoofed, or simply gone.

Positioning engine

Works out where a vehicle is from its own onboard sensors, on road or off, with no satellites, no cell signal, and no outside infrastructure.

Field app

Turn-by-turn navigation, mission planning, and TAK integration for crews, running fully offline on phones and rugged tablets.

Vehicle interface

Plug-and-play hardware that connects to the vehicle and streams what the engine needs, updated over the air.
